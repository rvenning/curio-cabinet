👥 Two Player

One device, two people, taking turns. Find a pair and you go again — which is how somebody wins four in a row and takes the whole thing. No clock: nobody should be hurried.

🎨 The Cabinet

Stars fit out the cabinet. A theme changes the table, the rim and the backs of the cards, and nothing else — none of them make the game any easier, which is the point of them.

🏆 The Family

Ranked on Time Attack, because it is the one mode where everybody plays the same way: thirty seconds on the clock, and every pair you find buys a little of it back.

📖 How to play

Every card has exactly one twin somewhere on the board. Turn over two: if they match they stay open, and if they do not, they go back down — but you got to see them, and that is the whole game.

  • Press, slide, lift. Nothing turns over until you take your finger off, so you can slide to the right drawer first.
  • A quick look. Every board flashes face-up before the clock starts. It gets shorter as you go deeper, and you will never hold all of it.
  • Stars come from misses, not from the clock. The clock only decides whether you finish; how well you finished is how few times you turned two that did not go together.
  • ⏱ Time Attack starts on thirty seconds. Every pair buys a bit back and clearing a board buys more, then a bigger one is dealt. It ends when the boards outgrow the clock — and that is the score on the family board.
